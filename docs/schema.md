### 1. What a Row Means (Entity Semantics)

* **`country`**: A recognized sovereign state and regional hub available for student nationality, passport issuance, or university hosting.
* **`exchange_cycle`**: A KU semester (`Fall` or `Spring` of an academic year written as `2026/2027`) with its windows for partner nominations and student applications and the dates the semester runs. Each window must open no later than it closes.
* **`kustudentauth`**: An enrolled Kuwait University student profile, linked to one Supabase Auth user and required to use an `@ku.edu.kw` email.
* **`incomingstudentauth`**: An international applicant profile, linked to one Supabase Auth user, used to prepare inbound exchange applications. `preferred_subject_codes` stores the student's preferred CBA courses as a set of catalog codes. It starts empty; saving requires at least one course and records `subject_preferences_created_at` on the first save and `subject_preferences_updated_at` whenever the set changes. These are registration preferences, not confirmed course registrations, and stay with the student when an application is replaced.
* **`emergency_contact`**: A personal safety contact (name, a phone number with 7 to 15 digits, optional email). Since #101 a contact belongs to one incoming student (`student_id`, at most one contact per student), who can save it before applying; the student's inbound applications point to it. Older contacts may have no `student_id` and are reached only through the application that points to them.
* **`partneruniversity`**: A university in the exchange programme, with its country, logo, description, map position and closest airport. Kuwait University itself is row `4`, the home institution; every other row is a partner. A partner is never deleted: deleting it from the portal retires it (`deleted_at` is set and `available` becomes false), so past applications keep their history while it disappears from listings and offers. Names are unique, ignoring letter case and surrounding spaces, among universities that are not retired.
* **`coordinator`**: A coordinator account linked to one Supabase Auth user and one university. A coordinator of Kuwait University (university `4`) with an `@ku.edu.kw` email is a KU coordinator, who runs the programme; every other coordinator is a partner coordinator, who nominates students. `email_accepted` records whether a partner coordinator's access was approved; `maintenance_mode` holds that coordinator's copy of the site-wide maintenance switch.
* **`studentnominations`**: A formal endorsement submitted by a partner coordinator sponsoring a foreign student for one KU semester. Its student email associates it with an incoming profile through a case-insensitive email match, whether the account exists before or after the nomination is created. The incoming application tab uses this association to prefill the newest active, available and unused nomination while allowing manual entry; it does not store a second nomination ID on the profile.
* **`inboundapplication`**: An incoming student's exchange application, made from a partner coordinator's nomination. Its status is one of `Draft`, `Submitted`, `In Review`, `Pending Interview`, `Accepted`, `Approved`, `Rejected`, `Ineligible`, `Withdrawal Requested`, `Withdrawn` or `Modified`. Editing a submitted application keeps the old version as `Modified` beside the edited copy, and `original_application_id` points every copy at the first version. `withdrawal_reason` and `withdrawal_previous_status` record a withdrawal request; `submitted_at` records when the student submitted.
* **Incoming approvals (#172)**: `approval_type` is `Conditional` or `Unconditional` after a decision. Conditional approval uses status `Accepted`; unconditional approval uses `Approved`. The portal treats older accepted or approved rows without an approval type as conditional until the coordinator verifies the certificate. A non-null approval type is allowed only on accepted, approved or historical modified rows. Unconditional approval requires `criminal_record_certificate` and the coordinator's explicit confirmation that its Kuwaiti embassy stamp was checked.
* **Application documents (#172)**: Both application tables store a private `cv` path. Incoming applications also store `english_evidence_type` (`IELTS`, `TOEFL` or `University letter`), an `english_evidence` path, and an optional `criminal_record_certificate` path. A university letter must confirm competent English and come from the dean, vice dean or head of department. Drafts may omit these files; new submissions require a CV and, for incoming students, the English evidence type and upload. The criminal-record certificate remains optional at submission and becomes necessary for unconditional approval. The health-insurance acknowledgement is informative UI state and has no database column.
* **`acceptance_letters` (#177)**: A Letter of Acceptance a KU coordinator issued for one incoming application. `letter_type` is `Conditional` or `Unconditional`, matching the application's `approval_type` when it was issued; an application has at most one letter of each type, so one that was upgraded can hold both. `file_path` is the generated, unstamped PDF in the `acceptance-letters` bucket (`storage_bucket`), with `issued_at` and the issuing coordinator's id, name and email. The coordinator signs and stamps the printed letter and uploads it; `stamped_file_path`, `stamped_at` and the uploading coordinator's id, name and email are then filled in together. Names and emails are copied onto the row so the audit trail survives a deleted coordinator account. The student receives only the stamped copy.
* **`inbound_schedule_items` (#180)**: One course in an incoming student's class schedule for their exchange semester, set by a KU coordinator on the student's `Accepted` or `Approved` inbound application. The semester, academic year and start and end dates come from the application's KU semester (`exchange_cycle`), not from the row. Each row holds the course's display code and name, the days it meets (`meeting_days`: weekday names, stored once each in week order from Sunday), its `start_time` and `end_time`, `room` and `instructor`; all are required, and the start is before the end. Times are Kuwait wall-clock times (Asia/Kuwait), so they carry no time zone. `subject_code` links a course on the CBA list to `subjects`, whose display code and name are copied onto the row each time it is saved; a course outside the CBA list leaves it empty and the coordinator types the code and name. A course that meets at different times on different days is entered once per time. `student_id` is copied from the application. `credit_hours` (1 to 6, default 3, the credit hours shown for every CBA course since #97) weights the course in the semester GPA (#181).
* **`inbound_course_grades` (#181)**: The letter grade a KU coordinator recorded for one scheduled course, at most one per course (`schedule_item_id` is the key). `letter_grade` is one of KU's letters `A`, `A-`, `B+`, `B`, `B-`, `C+`, `C`, `C-`, `D+`, `D` or `F`; the portal turns it into grade points on KU's 4.0 scale (A 4.00 down to F 0.00, D the lowest passing grade), so points are not stored. The application and student are copied from the course. `graded_at` and `graded_by` record when, and by which coordinator, the letter was last changed. A course with no row has no grade yet and is left out of the semester GPA, which is the credit-hour-weighted average of the graded courses' points and is calculated in the portal.
* **`outboundapplication`**: A Kuwait University student's application to study at a partner university for one KU semester, with the same statuses, versioning and withdrawal columns as inbound applications.
* **`outboundapplication.civil_id_copy`**: Stores the private storage path for the student's uploaded Civil ID document.
* **`outbound_course_choices` (#99)**: One KU course proposed on an outbound application. `id` is an identity key, `application_id` identifies the application, and optional `subject_code` links a CBA catalog course. The catalog display code and name are copied into `course_display_code` (up to 30 characters) and `course_title` (up to 255); custom courses supply both instead. `credits` is a finite positive decimal, never zero, negative, infinity or NaN. `created_at` records the first save and `updated_at` the latest save. The application may have at most eight rows. Following the student's clarification, editing is available on `Submitted`, `In Review` and `Pending Interview` applications; application approval (`Accepted` or `Approved`) locks the list. There is no separate course-list approval. This replaces the issue's conflicting instruction to show an editable form only after approval.
* **`application_status_log`**: An audit record for exactly one inbound or outbound application, with the old and new statuses, actor, timestamp, and optional reason. A trigger snapshots the actor's name when the record is created.
* **`updates`**: A message from a KU coordinator about exactly one application, inbound or outbound, or the office's answer to exactly one support request, with non-empty details and its creation time. Students see the updates on their own applications and support requests in their inbox.
* **`outbound_offers`**: An alternative partner university a KU coordinator offers an outbound applicant instead of their preferred choice, with an optional note kept exactly as typed. An offer is `Pending` until the student accepts or declines it or the coordinator withdraws it; an application has at most one pending offer per university.
* **`feedback`**: Non-empty feedback linked to exactly one inbound or outbound application, with its creation time and an edited flag.
* **`support_request`**: A question an incoming student sends to the Student Exchange Office: a category (`Application`, `Documents`, `Visa and residency`, `Housing`, `Courses and credits` or `Other`), a subject of up to 150 characters, a message of up to 1000 characters, and the storage paths of up to three attached files. Subject, message and reply are stored exactly as typed. A request is `Open` until the office replies; the single reply, its time and the coordinator who wrote it are saved on the request, which then becomes `Answered`. A request is `Answered` exactly when it has a reply. A student can have at most two `Open` requests at a time. The portal shows the request's `id` as its reference number (`SR-000042`).
* **`subjects`**: A College of Business Administration course imported from the CBA syllabus site: its seven-digit course code (from which the course's link on that site is built), display code, name, department, description, learning outcomes in order, and the storage path of its syllabus.
* **`exchange_program_info` (#102)**: A single row (`id = 1`) holding the public About page copy: the exchange team's description, the director's name and email, the assistant's name, email and phone (`+` and 7 to 15 digits), the programme's and the college's Instagram handles, the college's `https://…ku.edu.kw` web address, its accreditation and mailing address, and `additional_contacts`, a JSON list of further team members, each with a non-empty `name` and `title`. `updated_at` records the last change.
* **`testing`**: Row `id = 1` is the testing-mode switch and lists the synthetic demo accounts; every other row registers exactly one record or uploaded file created while testing mode was on.
* **`testing_backup`**: A copy of a real partner university or coordinator as it was before testing mode first changed it, so ending testing mode can restore it. Only database functions read or write it.

### 2. Entity Relationships (Business Rules & Constraints)

* **`partneruniversity` -> `country`**: Identifies the country the university is in (`ON DELETE SET NULL`).
* **`coordinator` -> `partneruniversity`**: Binds a coordinator to their university (`ON DELETE CASCADE`). Retiring a university keeps its row, so its coordinators stay, but they lose access.
* **`studentnominations` -> `coordinator`**: Attributes each nomination to the coordinator who submitted it (`ON DELETE CASCADE`).
* **`studentnominations` -> `exchange_cycle`**: Anchors a nomination to one KU semester (`ON DELETE CASCADE`).
* **`inboundapplication` -> `studentnominations`**: Ties an inbound application to the nomination it was made from. There is no `ON DELETE` rule, so a nomination that has an application cannot be deleted, and neither can its coordinator, whose deletion would delete the nomination. A nomination can back only one application that is not `Rejected` or `Modified`, so a rejected student may apply again with the same nomination.
* **`inboundapplication` -> `incomingstudentauth`**: Ties an inbound application to the applicant's account (`ON DELETE SET NULL`). A student can have only one application per KU semester that is not `Rejected` or `Modified`.
* **`inboundapplication` -> `partneruniversity`**: Records the student's home institution, which a trigger copies from the university of the coordinator who issued the nomination (`ON DELETE SET NULL`).
* **`inboundapplication` -> `exchange_cycle`**: Places the application in the nomination's KU semester (`ON DELETE RESTRICT`), so a semester that inbound applications point to cannot be deleted.
* **`inboundapplication` -> `country` (Nationality / Passport Issuing)**: Records the applicant's citizenship and the country that issued their passport (`ON DELETE SET NULL`).
* **`inboundapplication` -> `emergency_contact`**: Connects the applicant to a designated emergency contact (`ON DELETE SET NULL`). The `inboundapplication_set_emergency_contact` trigger points a new application, or one whose student changes, at that student's saved contact.
* **`emergency_contact` -> `incomingstudentauth`**: The student the contact belongs to, at most one contact per student; deleting the student's profile deletes the contact (`ON DELETE CASCADE`).
* **`acceptance_letters` -> `inboundapplication` / `incomingstudentauth`**: The application the letter is for and the student who receives it; deleting either deletes the letter (`ON DELETE CASCADE`). `acceptance_letters_one_per_type` allows one letter of each type per application.
* **`acceptance_letters` -> `coordinator` (`issued_by`, `stamped_by`)**: The KU coordinators who issued the letter and uploaded its stamped copy (`ON DELETE SET NULL`; their copied names and emails remain).
* **`inbound_schedule_items` -> `inboundapplication` / `incomingstudentauth`**: The application the course was scheduled on and the student who takes it; deleting either deletes the course (`ON DELETE CASCADE`). The student is copied from the application when the course is added, and a course cannot be moved to another application.
* **`inbound_schedule_items` -> `subjects` (`subject_code`)**: The CBA course, when the course is on the CBA list (`ON DELETE SET NULL`; the copied code and name remain).
* **`inbound_course_grades` -> `inbound_schedule_items` / `inboundapplication` / `incomingstudentauth`**: The graded course, its application and its student; deleting any of them deletes the grade (`ON DELETE CASCADE`). The application and student are copied from the course, and a grade cannot be moved to another course.
* **`inbound_course_grades` -> `coordinator` (`graded_by`)**: The KU coordinator who last changed the letter (`ON DELETE SET NULL`).
* **`outboundapplication` -> `kustudentauth`**: Ties an outbound application to the Kuwait University student's account (`ON DELETE SET NULL`). A student can have only one application per KU semester that is not `Rejected` or `Modified`.
* **`outboundapplication` -> `partneruniversity`**: The host university the student prefers (`ON DELETE SET NULL`).
* **`outboundapplication` -> `exchange_cycle`**: The KU semester the student applies for (`ON DELETE SET NULL`).
* **`outbound_course_choices` -> `outboundapplication` / `subjects`**: Deleting the application deletes its choices (`ON DELETE CASCADE`). Deleting a catalog subject clears its link (`ON DELETE SET NULL`) while retaining the saved code, title and credits. The `(application_id, id)` index supports reading a list in save order. Editing an application through `modify_outbound_application` copies the choices to the new version and preserves the old version's list and saved timestamps as history.
* **`outboundapplication` -> `country` (Nationality / Passport Issuing)**: Records the student's citizenship and the country that issued their passport (`ON DELETE SET NULL`).
* **`inboundapplication` / `outboundapplication` -> itself (`original_application_id`)**: Points every edited copy at the first version, so all versions of one application share it (`ON DELETE SET NULL`).
* **`inboundapplication` / `outboundapplication` -> `updates` (`update_id`)**: An older optional link from an application to a single update (`ON DELETE SET NULL`).
* **`updates` -> `inboundapplication` / `outboundapplication` / `support_request`**: Each update belongs to exactly one application or exactly one support request (`updates_one_subject`); deleting that application or request deletes its updates (`ON DELETE CASCADE`).
* **`application_status_log` -> `inboundapplication` / `outboundapplication`**: Associates each audit row with exactly one application; deleting that application removes its log entries (`ON DELETE CASCADE`).
* **`outbound_offers` -> `outboundapplication`**: The application the offer is for; deleting it deletes its offers (`ON DELETE CASCADE`).
* **`outbound_offers` -> `partneruniversity`**: The university offered (`ON DELETE CASCADE`).
* **`outbound_offers` -> `coordinator`**: The KU coordinator who made the offer (`ON DELETE SET NULL`).
* **`feedback` -> `inboundapplication` / `outboundapplication`**: Associates each feedback row with exactly one application; deleting that application removes its feedback (`ON DELETE CASCADE`).
* **`support_request` -> `incomingstudentauth`**: The incoming student who sent the request; deleting the student's profile deletes their requests (`ON DELETE CASCADE`).
* **`support_request` -> `coordinator`**: The KU coordinator who replied (`ON DELETE SET NULL`).
* **`kustudentauth` / `incomingstudentauth` / `coordinator` -> `auth.users`**: Connects each profile to its Supabase Auth identity, one profile per user. There is no `ON DELETE` rule, so an Auth user cannot be deleted while a profile still points to it; `delete_my_account` deletes the profile first and then the Auth user.
* **`coordinator.email_accepted`**: Starts `false`. The sign-up page creates KU coordinators already accepted and partner coordinators as access requests, which a KU coordinator approves. The database does not check this value at sign-up: the insert policy only requires that the profile belongs to the signed-in user and uses their email. A KU coordinator cannot approve a coordinator whose university is retired.
* **`testing`**: Each row other than `id = 1` references exactly one public row or uploaded file created during testing mode, and is deleted with it (`ON DELETE CASCADE`).
* **`testing_backup`**: Keyed by table name and record id; it has no foreign keys.

### 3. Row-Level Security Policies (Who, What & Why)

Row Level Security is on for all 23 tables. The maintenance-mode and testing-mode policies at the end of this section apply on top of every other policy.

* **`country` / `"Anyone can read countries"` (SELECT)**: Anonymous and authenticated users may read country records so forms and dropdowns populate without authentication barriers.
* **`exchange_cycle` / `"Users can read exchange cycles"` (SELECT)**: Signed-in KU students, incoming students and coordinators can read cycle dates so the portal can check application windows.
* **`exchange_cycle` / `"KU coordinators can create exchange cycles"` (INSERT)** and **`"KU coordinators can update exchange cycles"` (UPDATE)**: KU coordinators may create KU semesters and update their details.
* **`exchange_cycle` / `"KU coordinators can delete exchange cycles"` (DELETE)**: KU coordinators may permanently delete exchange cycles. Linked student nominations are removed by the foreign-key cascade; a semester that inbound applications point to cannot be deleted.
* **`kustudentauth` / `"Users can register their KU student profile"` (INSERT)**: Authenticated Kuwait University students may create a profile only when `user_id` matches `auth.uid()` and the email uses the KU domain.
* **`kustudentauth` / `"Users can read their KU student profile"` (SELECT)** and **`"KU coordinators can read KU students with applications"` (SELECT)**: A student reads their own profile; KU coordinators read the profiles of students who have an outbound application.
* **`kustudentauth` / `"Users can delete their KU student profile"` (DELETE)**: Authenticated Kuwait University students may delete only their own profile. Students change their details only through `update_my_ku_student_account`.
* **`incomingstudentauth` / `"Users can register their incoming student profile"` (INSERT)**: Authenticated international applicants may create a profile only when `user_id` matches `auth.uid()`. New profiles must have empty subject preferences and no preference timestamps, so registration cannot bypass the nomination check in `save_incoming_subject_preferences`.
* **`incomingstudentauth` / `"Users can read their incoming student profile"` (SELECT)** and **`"KU coordinators can read incoming students with applications"` (SELECT)**: A student reads their own profile; KU coordinators read the profiles of students who have an inbound application.
* **`incomingstudentauth` / `"Users can delete their incoming student profile"` (DELETE)**: Authenticated international applicants may delete only their own profile. Students change their details only through `update_my_incoming_student_account`.
* **`coordinator` / `"Users can register their coordinator profile"` (INSERT)**: Coordinators may create their own profile only when `user_id` and email match their authenticated identity. While testing mode is on, no new KU coordinator profile can be created.
* **`coordinator` / `"Coordinators can read coordinator profiles"` (SELECT)**: Coordinators read their own profile; KU coordinators read every profile.
* **`coordinator` / `"KU coordinators can update coordinator profiles"` (UPDATE)**: KU coordinators approve partner coordinators and change coordinator records, but cannot approve a coordinator whose university is retired. Coordinators change their own name and email only through `update_my_coordinator_account`.
* **`coordinator` / `"Users and KU coordinators can delete coordinator profiles"` (DELETE)**: Coordinators may delete their own record; KU coordinators may delete coordinator records.
* **`partneruniversity` / `"Anyone can read available partner universities"` (SELECT)**: Visitors and signed-in users can read universities that are available.
* **`partneruniversity` / `"Users can read available partner universities"` (SELECT)**: KU coordinators read every university, including retired ones; signed-in KU students read available ones.
* **`partneruniversity` / `"Coordinators can read their own university"` (SELECT)**: A coordinator can always read their own university, even when it is unavailable or retired.
* **`partneruniversity` / `"KU coordinators can add partner universities"` (INSERT)** and **`"KU coordinators can edit partner universities"` (UPDATE)**: KU coordinators add universities and edit any university except Kuwait University (row `4`) and retired ones. There is no delete policy; universities are retired through `retire_partner_university`.
* **`emergency_contact` / `"KU coordinators can read emergency contacts"` (SELECT)** and **`"Incoming students can read their own emergency contact"` (SELECT)**: KU coordinators read every emergency contact; an incoming student reads only the contact that belongs to them. No policy lets anyone create or change a contact; students save theirs through `save_my_emergency_contact` (section 4).
* **`studentnominations` / `"Coordinators can create student nominations"` (INSERT)**: A coordinator may create nominations only when `coordinator_id` matches the coordinator profile linked to their authenticated user, and only while their university is not retired.
* **`studentnominations` / `"Coordinators can read student nominations"` (SELECT)**: Coordinators may view nominations assigned to their profile; KU coordinators may view all nominations.
* **`studentnominations` / `"Nominated students can read their nominations"` (SELECT)**: An authenticated incoming student may read only nominations addressed to their profile email so they can validate their Nomination ID.
* **`studentnominations` / `"Coordinators can update their student nominations"` (UPDATE)** and **`"Coordinators can delete their student nominations"` (DELETE)**: A coordinator may edit their own nominations while their university is not retired, and delete their own nominations.
* **`studentnominations.id`**: Each nomination uses a unique, randomly generated eight-digit identifier that coordinators share with the nominated student. The portal generates it; the database only requires a positive number.
* **`studentnominations.nomination_status`**: New nominations default to `Active`. `expire_exchange_cycle_nominations` changes them to `Expired` once their KU semester has ended, but only when a KU coordinator runs it: nothing schedules it and the portal does not call it.
* **`inboundapplication` / `"Incoming students can read their inbound applications"` (SELECT)**: A student reads their own applications.
* **`inboundapplication` / `"Incoming students can create inbound application drafts"` (INSERT)**: A student creates a draft only for their own profile and email, from an `Active` nomination addressed to that email, for the nomination's KU semester, while that semester's application window is open and before the semester ends.
* **`inboundapplication` / `"Incoming students can update their inbound application drafts"` (UPDATE)**: A student edits their own draft or submits it (status `Submitted` with the terms accepted), under the same nomination and window conditions.
* **`inboundapplication` / `"Incoming students can delete their inbound application drafts"` (DELETE)**: A student may delete only their own drafts.
* **`inboundapplication` / `"KU coordinators can read inbound applications"` (SELECT)** and **`"KU coordinators can read inbound application drafts"` (SELECT)**: KU coordinators read every inbound application, drafts included.
* **`inboundapplication` / `"Partner coordinators can read applications for own nominations"` (SELECT)**: Approved partner coordinators can read applications, drafts included, linked to their own nominations. This is row-level access, not column-level masking; progress clients request `nomination_id`, `application_status` and `approval_type` to display the approval stage.
* **`inboundapplication` / `"KU coordinators can link updates to inbound applications"` (UPDATE)**: KU coordinators may change an application's `update_id`; a trigger rejects any other direct change. Decisions, withdrawals and edits go through the functions in section 4.
* **`inboundapplication` drafts**: Drafts may omit names until the student completes them; submission requires first and last names. `submitted_at` records the submission time for the student receipt and PDF export.
* **`acceptance_letters` / `"KU coordinators can read acceptance letters"` (SELECT)** and **`"Incoming students can read their stamped acceptance letters"` (SELECT)**: KU coordinators read every letter; an incoming student reads only their own letters that have a stamped copy, and only while the letter's application is `Accepted` or `Approved` (#132), so an unstamped letter stays with the office and a withdrawn or no-longer-approved application loses access. No policy lets anyone write a letter; issuing and stamping go through the functions in section 4.
* **`inbound_schedule_items` / `"KU coordinators can read schedules"` (SELECT)** and **`"Incoming students can read their own schedule"` (SELECT)**: KU coordinators read every schedule; an incoming student reads only their own courses, and only while the application they were set on is `Accepted` or `Approved` (#180).
* **`inbound_schedule_items` / `"KU coordinators can add schedule courses"` (INSERT)**, **`"KU coordinators can edit schedule courses"` (UPDATE)** and **`"KU coordinators can remove schedule courses"` (DELETE)**: KU coordinators add courses to, and edit courses on, applications that are `Accepted` or `Approved`, and remove courses. Students cannot change their schedule. All three refuse during maintenance mode and, during testing mode, accept only test applications (`testing_change_allowed`); a test application's courses are deleted with it when testing ends, so the table has no testing tracker of its own.
* **`inbound_course_grades` / `"KU coordinators can read grades"` (SELECT)** and **`"Incoming students can read their own grades"` (SELECT)**: KU coordinators read every grade. An incoming student reads only their own grades, and only once the stamped acceptance letter for that application has been sent to them and while the application is `Accepted` or `Approved` (#181), so the schedule can be shown before the letter but the grades cannot.
* **`inbound_course_grades` / `"KU coordinators can record grades"` (INSERT)**, **`"KU coordinators can change grades"` (UPDATE)** and **`"KU coordinators can remove grades"` (DELETE)**: KU coordinators record and change grades on applications that are `Accepted` or `Approved`, and remove grades. Students cannot change grades. As with schedules, all three refuse during maintenance mode and, during testing mode, accept only test applications; a test application's grades are deleted with its courses.
* **`outboundapplication` / `"Users can read their outbound applications"` (SELECT)**: A student reads their own applications; KU coordinators read every outbound application.
* **`outboundapplication` / `"KU students can create outbound application drafts"` (INSERT)**: A student with a KU email creates a draft only for their own profile, for a KU semester whose application window has not closed.
* **`outboundapplication` / `"KU students can update their outbound applications"` (UPDATE)**: A student edits their own draft or submits it (status `Submitted` with the terms accepted) while the semester's application window has not closed.
* **`outboundapplication` / `"KU students can delete their outbound application drafts"` (DELETE)**: A student may delete only their own drafts.
* **`outboundapplication` / `"KU coordinators can link updates to outbound applications"` (UPDATE)**: KU coordinators may change an application's `update_id`; a trigger rejects any other direct change.
* **`outbound_course_choices` / `"Students and KU coordinators can read outbound course choices"` (SELECT)**: The application-owning KU student and KU coordinators read the list; other students and partner coordinators cannot. Anonymous users have no access. Authenticated users have SELECT permission only; inserts, edits and deletes must use the guarded functions below. `"Maintenance blocks outbound course choice reads"` hides all choices when maintenance is on or its state is unknown. Testing-mode writes follow the application's testing registration, and choices are deleted with their test application, so this table needs no testing tracker.
* **`application_status_log` / `"KU students and coordinators can read application status logs"` (SELECT)**: Application-owning KU and incoming students and KU coordinators may read status history. No policy lets users write it; the database functions in section 4 add its rows.
* **`updates` / update policies**: KU coordinators may read every update, create updates and delete them; students may read the updates on their own applications and their own support requests. Update creation, changes, and deletion are also restricted to records registered for the active testing mode.
* **`outbound_offers` / `"KU coordinators and applicants can read outbound offers"` (SELECT)**: KU coordinators and the student who owns the application can read offers. Offers are created, answered and withdrawn only through the functions in section 4.
* **`feedback` / feedback policies**: Anyone may read feedback; authenticated application authors may create and update feedback on their own applications, and only application authors or KU coordinators may delete it.
* **`support_request` / support request policies**: An incoming student may read only their own requests and may create a request for themselves that is `Open`, has no reply, and whose attached files sit in their own storage folder. KU coordinators may read every request. No policy lets anyone change or delete a request; the office's reply goes through `answer_support_request` (section 4). Creation is also limited by testing mode like every other table.
* **`subjects` / `"Signed-in users can read subjects"` (SELECT)**: Every signed-in user can read the CBA course list.
* **`subjects` / `"KU coordinators can add subjects"` (INSERT)** and **`"KU coordinators can update subjects"` (UPDATE)**: KU coordinators import and update courses, outside maintenance mode. No policy lets anyone delete a course.
* **`exchange_program_info` / `"Visitors can read exchange program information"` (SELECT)**: Visitors and signed-in users read the About page copy, including during maintenance mode.
* **`exchange_program_info` / `"KU coordinators can publish exchange program information"` (INSERT)** and **`"KU coordinators can edit exchange program information"` (UPDATE)**: KU coordinators create and edit the row, but not during maintenance mode or testing mode. No policy lets anyone delete it.
* **`inbound-application-files` (private bucket; PDF, JPEG or PNG up to 10 MB)**: Files sit in a `<student_id>/<application_id>/<field>/` folder. Students upload to their own application while it is a draft, submitted, in review or pending interview, read their own files, and delete draft files. Conditional applicants may upload only to `criminal_record_certificate/`; they save its path through `save_inbound_criminal_record`. They may delete an unlinked certificate upload after a failed save, but cannot delete a certificate referenced by an application. Unconditional approval prevents further student certificate uploads. KU coordinators read every file and upload replacements for applications that are submitted, in review or pending interview.
* **`outbound-application-files` (private bucket; PDF, JPEG or PNG up to 10 MB)**: Students upload, read and delete files in their own application folders. KU coordinators read every file and upload replacements for applications that are submitted, in review or pending interview.
* **`partner-university-logos` (public bucket; PNG or JPEG up to 2 MB)**: KU coordinators upload logos; anyone can view them through their public links.
* **`syllabi` (private bucket; PDF or Word up to 10 MB)**: KU coordinators upload and replace syllabi. KU coordinators, incoming students with a nomination, and incoming students with an accepted application can download them (`can_download_syllabi`).
* **`support-request-files` (private bucket; PDF, JPEG or PNG up to 10 MB)**: Files sit in a `<student_id>/<upload_id>/` folder. Incoming students upload to and read their own folder, and may delete a file only while no request lists it, which lets the form clean up when saving a request fails. KU coordinators read every file.
* **`acceptance-letters` (private bucket; PDF only, up to 10 MB since #132)**: Files sit in a `<student_id>/<application_id>/<conditional|unconditional>/` folder. KU coordinators upload and read every file, and may delete a file only while no letter points at it, which lets the page clean up when issuing or stamping fails. An incoming student reads only the stamped copies of their own letters, under the same approved-application condition as the table.
* **`testing` / mode and record policies**: Anyone may read control row `id = 1`, and KU coordinators may read every tracker row; KU coordinators change testing mode and save synthetic credentials through guarded functions. A trigger registers every inserted public-data or storage row while testing mode is active, and users cannot add tracker rows themselves. Testing-mode restrictions limit updates or deletes to registered rows.
* **`testing_backup` / `"Testing backups stay private"` (ALL, restrictive)**: Anonymous and authenticated clients cannot read or change backup rows, including KU coordinators. Only the guarded owner functions can capture and restore the original records.
* **Maintenance mode**: Restrictive policies hide every table's rows while maintenance mode is on, or while its status is unknown; the exceptions are a KU coordinator's own coordinator profile, so they can sign in and use the maintenance toggle, and `exchange_program_info`, which has no such policy. Writes are refused at the same times, except the maintenance switch itself.
* **`testing.test_accounts`**: This field contains runtime-generated credentials for synthetic accounts and is intentionally public so visitors can switch roles without entering credentials. Setup creates a KU student with an outbound application, an incoming student with a nomination and inbound application, a partner coordinator, and a second KU student without an application. These accounts are limited by RLS to testing records; no real account credentials belong here.
* **`testing` / cleanup**: Ending testing mode removes tracked application rows, generated Auth profiles/accounts, and tracked uploads, and restores real partner universities and coordinators from `testing_backup`. Upload bytes are removed through the Supabase Storage API before database cleanup completes; `"KU coordinators can remove registered testing files"` lets KU coordinators delete only files registered during the test.

### 4. Database Functions, Triggers & Scheduled Jobs

These functions run with the database owner's rights and check the caller themselves, so they can change rows that no policy in section 3 lets users change directly. Those that change application data refuse during maintenance mode and, during testing mode, change only records created in the test.

* **Account details**: `update_my_ku_student_account`, `update_my_incoming_student_account` and `update_my_coordinator_account` let a signed-in user change their own name and email, and KU students their student ID. `delete_my_account` deletes the caller's profile and then their Auth user.
* **Subject preferences (#159)**: `can_select_incoming_subjects` checks whether the signed-in incoming student has a nomination addressed to their profile email or an inbound application with status `Accepted` or `Approved`, matching the existing syllabus-access rule. `save_incoming_subject_preferences` saves only that student's preferences, validates every code against `subjects`, removes duplicates and records the first-save and last-modified times on the server. Saving the same set again leaves both times unchanged. Saves refuse during maintenance and, during testing, for profiles not registered as testing records. Preferences inherit the profile's existing read policies and are removed with its testing-account cleanup; no new table or testing tracker is needed.
* **Decisions**: `review_outbound_application` lets a KU coordinator accept or reject a pending outbound application. `review_inbound_application(requested_application_id, accept_application, update_details, unconditional_approval, certificate_stamped)` rejects or conditionally approves a pending incoming application; unconditional approval additionally checks the uploaded certificate exists in the student's private storage and requires `certificate_stamped = true`. It also upgrades a conditional application to unconditional approval. Existing three-argument callers still produce conditional approval through default arguments. The decision and stamp verification are logged in `application_status_log`, and an update gives the student the coordinator's text or the default next steps. The database records the human verification; it does not detect embassy stamps in file contents.
* **Certificate after conditional approval**: `save_inbound_criminal_record` lets only the applicant attach a certificate to their own conditional application. It checks the application, mode, storage object and application-specific path while locking the application row. It changes no approval state. A KU coordinator must subsequently verify the stamp and grant unconditional approval.
* **Acceptance letters (#177)**: `issue_acceptance_letter(requested_application_id, requested_letter_type, requested_file_path)` lets a KU coordinator record a letter whose type matches the application's current approval, once per type, after checking that the PDF was uploaded to that application's folder for that type. It records the issuing coordinator and sends the student nothing. `save_stamped_acceptance_letter(requested_letter_id, requested_file_path)` records the stamped copy once, from the same folder, and adds an update to the student's inbox saying the letter can be downloaded. During testing mode both accept only test applications; a letter on a test application is removed with it when testing ends, so the table has no tracker of its own.
* **Required documents**: `inboundapplication_validate_documents` and `outboundapplication_validate_documents` reject new submissions without the required CV, incoming English evidence type and incoming English upload. Original submissions must reference existing storage objects in their own field folders. Guarded version-edit functions validate replacements and may retain files from previous versions. Existing submitted records are not retroactively rejected by migration. The sign-in demonstration applications receive uploaded synthetic PDFs before submission. The guarded graph-data generator creates separate account-free synthetic records directly in later statuses; those records have submission dates for graphing but no documents and are not complete student-submitted forms.
* **Withdrawals**: `request_outbound_withdrawal` and `request_inbound_withdrawal` let the student who owns a `Submitted` or `Pending Interview` application ask to withdraw it, with an optional reason. `review_outbound_withdrawal` and `review_inbound_withdrawal` let a KU coordinator approve the request or reject it, which restores the previous status.
* **Emergency contacts (#101)**: `save_my_emergency_contact(requested_name, requested_phone, requested_email)` lets the signed-in incoming student create or replace their own contact (a name of up to 255 characters, a phone number written as `+` and 7 to 15 digits, an optional email) and points their current applications at it. `find_emergency_contacts(search_term)` lets a KU coordinator, outside maintenance mode, search incoming students by name or email (up to 50 results) and returns each one's own contact, or else the contact on their latest application.
* **Class schedules (#180)**: `inbound_schedule_items_prepare` (trigger) copies the student from the application when a course is added, refuses to move a course to another application, copies the code and name of a CBA course from `subjects`, and stores the meeting days once each in week order. Courses are written directly under the policies in section 3; there is no schedule function.
* **Course grades (#181)**: `inbound_course_grades_prepare` (trigger) copies the application and student from the graded course, refuses to move a grade to another course, and records the time and the signed-in coordinator whenever the letter changes. Grades are written directly under the policies in section 3.
* **Outbound course choices (#99)**: `save_outbound_course_choice(requested_application_id, requested_choice_id, requested_subject_code, requested_course_code, requested_course_title, requested_credits)` creates a row when the choice id is null, otherwise edits a row on that application. `delete_outbound_course_choice(requested_application_id, requested_choice_id)` removes a row. Both check the signed-in applicant, maintenance/testing restrictions and the pre-decision status, and lock the application row. The same lock is used for application decisions, so approval cannot race a course save and concurrent additions cannot exceed eight. Saving validates positive finite credits, required code/title and a real catalog reference; catalog titles and codes come from `subjects`, never from the caller. Neither function allows coordinators to edit a student's choices.
* **Support requests**: `answer_support_request` lets a KU coordinator reply to an `Open` support request with non-empty text. In one step it saves the reply exactly as typed, marks the request `Answered`, and adds the reply to the student's inbox as an update, so a request is answered only once.
* **`support_request_limit_open` (trigger)**: Refuses a new support request while the student already has two `Open` ones. It locks the student's profile row while counting, so two requests sent at the same moment cannot both get through.
* **Edits**: `modify_outbound_application` and `modify_inbound_application` let the student or a KU coordinator edit an application that is `Submitted`, `In Review` or `Pending Interview`. The edited copy replaces it, and the old version becomes `Modified`.
* **Alternative offers**: `offer_outbound_alternatives` lets a KU coordinator offer one or more universities for an application that is `Submitted` or `Pending Interview`. `respond_to_outbound_offer` lets the applicant decline an offer, or accept it while the application is still `Submitted` or `Pending Interview`; accepting makes the offered university the application's university and declines the application's other pending offers. `withdraw_outbound_offer` lets a KU coordinator withdraw a pending offer.
* **Partner universities**: `retire_partner_university` lets a KU coordinator retire any university except Kuwait University, outside maintenance mode; its coordinators' `email_accepted` becomes false.
* **Maintenance mode**: `get_maintenance_mode` is created before its first dependent function or policy. It returns `true` or `false` when every coordinator row agrees and `NULL` when they disagree, which the portal shows as an unknown status. `set_maintenance_mode` lets a KU coordinator switch it on every coordinator row at once.
* **Testing mode**: `set_testing_mode`, `save_testing_accounts`, `get_testing_storage_objects` and `end_testing_mode` let KU coordinators start testing mode, store the synthetic accounts, list test uploads for removal, and end testing mode. The testing page's **Fill cycle graphs with demo data** action can also populate an already-running scenario or retry graph generation; it selects the latest registered test cycle whose applications have opened and calls the same idempotent generator used during setup.
* **Testing graph data (requested alongside #99)**: `seed_testing_cycle_applications(requested_cycle_id)` is executable only by signed-in KU coordinators while testing mode is on and maintenance is off, and only for a cycle registered in the current test. Setup calls it after preparing the five role-switching accounts and their demonstration applications. It adds 80 incoming and 80 outgoing account-free applications with invented names, varied statuses, nationalities, available partner universities, GPAs, passed credits, and submission times between the cycle's opening and the earlier of its closing or now. Setup opens the synthetic application window 56 days earlier, making daily and weekly graphs populated. Synthetic partner-coordinator and nomination rows give incoming graph records their home university. The testing control row is locked during seeding; a repeated call for the same seeded cycle does nothing. No real application is changed, no additional Auth accounts or uploads are created, and all generated rows enter the existing testing registry for cleanup. Ending testing mode also cascades deletion of test course choices.
* **Nomination expiry**: `expire_exchange_cycle_nominations` lets a KU coordinator mark nominations `Expired` once their KU semester has ended. Nothing runs it automatically.
* **Scheduled job `reject-unsubmitted-applications`** (every minute, `pg_cron`): Rejects inbound and outbound drafts whose application window has closed, logging each change as made by `System` with the reason "Not submitted before the application deadline". It does nothing during maintenance mode.
* **`inboundapplication_validate_dates` / `outboundapplication_validate_dates` (triggers)**: Reject a birthdate that is not in the past, an expired passport or Civil ID, and an expected graduation date that is not in the future.
* **`inboundapplication_set_university` (trigger)**: Copies an inbound application's home university from the nomination's coordinator.
* **`inboundapplication_restrict_coordinator_update` / `outboundapplication_restrict_coordinator_update` (triggers)**: Only KU coordinators may change `update_id`, and outside the decision and withdrawal functions it is the only column they may change directly.
* **`application_status_log_snapshot_actor_name` (trigger)**: Saves the name of the person who changed the status with each log row.
* **`exchange_program_info_updated_at` (trigger)**: Sets `updated_at` whenever the About page copy is saved. `valid_exchange_team_contacts` checks each entry of `additional_contacts`.
* **Testing triggers**: `*_track_testing_insert` on storage and on every public table except `outbound_offers`, `outbound_course_choices`, `subjects`, `acceptance_letters`, `inbound_schedule_items`, `inbound_course_grades`, `exchange_program_info`, `testing` and `testing_backup` registers new rows while testing mode is on (offers, course choices, letters, schedule courses and grades are deleted with their test application; subjects and the About page copy are not test data); `partneruniversity_snapshot_before_testing_change` saves a real university to `testing_backup` before testing first changes it; `testing_registry_insert_guard` stops users adding tracker rows directly.
