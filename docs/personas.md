# Personas

Two to four people who will use your system. Real roles, not demographics.

One paragraph each: who they are, what they are trying to get done, and what makes it hard
today. A persona you cannot picture doing their job is not useful.

You write these in Phase 2, from what your client told you — not from imagination.

---

## **Areej, Exchange Program Coordinator**

Areej manages all outbound and inbound student exchange applications for the College of Business Administration. She spends her days buried in emails and paper forms, manually copying student grades, credit hours, and university preferences into a messy Excel sheet. 

During peak application season, she constantly worries about making a data entry error or losing an application in her overflowing inbox, which has happened before. She manually checks if each student meets the GPA and credit requirements, knowing that every unqualified applicant who slips through to the interview stage is a waste of time for both the faculty and a deserving, qualified student.
