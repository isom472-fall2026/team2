## What was delivered

By the end of Phase 5, incoming students could do much more of their exchange through the portal. They can send the Student Exchange Office a support request and read the reply in their inbox, save an emergency contact that KU coordinators can look up, pick the CBA subjects they would like to register for, and read a pre-arrival guide covering visas, accommodation, arrival, the campus and living in Kuwait. KU coordinators can now issue conditional and unconditional Letters of Acceptance and send the stamped copy, which the student downloads from their application. Once an incoming student is approved, a KU coordinator can set their class schedule and record their grades. The student sees the schedule by day in Kuwait time and can download it as a PDF. They can also read how KU's grading scale and GPA work, and once their stamped letter has been sent, see their grades and semester GPA and download a grade report. Outgoing students can list the KU courses they plan to take abroad until their application is approved. KU coordinators can see graphs that summarise an exchange cycle and compare two cycles side by side, and visitors can read a public page about the exchange team and the College of Business Administration. Alongside the stories, the Phase 4 fixes were made (#172): conditional and unconditional approval of incoming applications, a criminal-record certificate checked for its Kuwaiti embassy stamp, and required CVs and English evidence. `main` was also restored to the Phase 4 snapshot and the later pull requests were reapplied in order (#164, #166). The Manage button on the coordinator's application lists now stays in reach on narrow screens, testing mode fills the cycle graphs with demo applications, the client's written approval of our scope changes was recorded in [scope-changes.md](../scope-changes.md), and [schema.md](../schema.md) was brought in line with [schema.sql](../../db/schema.sql).
