Program Details

Everything students need to understand the exchange program before starting an application.

✓

Eligibility

Open to undergraduate and postgraduate students who have completed at least one academic year at Kuwait University with a minimum CGPA of 2.5.

◷

Duration

Exchange periods last one full semester or one academic year, aligned with the host institution's academic calendar.

▦

Credit Transfer

Courses taken abroad are reviewed by the Academic Council and, when equivalent, transferred to the KU transcript.

₍₎

Financial Support

Selected students may be eligible for KU travel grants and fee waivers under bilateral agreements.

Program rules

  • Choose a partner university that is available for your program and exchange period.
  • Submit the application and supporting documents before the published deadline.
  • Courses taken abroad are subject to academic review and credit-transfer procedures.
  • Students must follow both Kuwait University rules and the host institution's regulations.
  • Keep your contact information and application documents up to date.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most from students and coordinators.

What GPA and credits do I need to apply?

A minimum CGPA of 2.5, at least 60 completed credits, and 4 completed semesters at Kuwait University.

How long does a decision take?

Applications are reviewed in the order they are received. Eligible applicants are typically contacted for an interview within two to three weeks of the deadline.

How does the inbound process work for partner students?

A coordinator at the partner university submits the nomination on the student's behalf. KU reviews the program fit, then confirms the placement and sends housing and orientation details.

Will my credits transfer back to my KU transcript?

Courses taken abroad are reviewed by the Academic Council and transferred when equivalent to a KU course.

What documents do I need to submit?

An unofficial transcript, proof of English proficiency, and your three preferred host universities in order of preference.

Can I apply to more than one partner university?

Yes — every application includes three ranked choices, and the office places you at the best available match.
