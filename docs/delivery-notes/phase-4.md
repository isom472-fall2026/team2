## What was delivered

By the end of Phase 4, KU coordinators could review outgoing and incoming student applications and accept or reject them, with the student seeing the new status in their portal. Coordinators could send students updates about their applications, which students read in an Inbox tab, and coordinators and incoming students could edit an application while it was still awaiting a decision. Coordinators could filter the application lists by status or GPA and offer an outbound applicant alternative partner universities when their first choice was unavailable. KU coordinators could also add partner universities to the website and edit or delete them; a deleted university disappears from the directory and from applications, and its partner coordinators lose access. CBA subjects and their syllabi can now be imported from the KU source website, and incoming students and KU coordinators can browse, search and filter KU courses and their syllabi. Students can update their own details after signing up. Alongside the stories, KU coordinators can put the website in testing mode (#125), the home page was redesigned around a glowing Earth globe with a sky-blue light mode (#129), and the fixes required after Phase 3 were made (#127, #134). and we added the scope changes section to each issue.
